Commands

All commands live under /ic. Type /ic in any channel to get started.

Account Management

/ic link ✓ Available

Link your Discord account to a FACEIT nickname. The bot verifies it's a CS2 account and stores the stable player ID.

/ic link faceit:YourFaceitNickname
/ic unlink ✓ Available

Remove your FACEIT account link.

/ic unlink
/ic linkstatus ✓ Available

View a linked FACEIT account with current live ELO and level.

/ic linkstatus /ic linkstatus user:@Adam
/ic adminunlink ✓ Available

Remove another user's FACEIT account link. Server administrators only.

/ic adminunlink user:@Adam

Player Statistics

/ic stats ✓ Available

Lifetime stats — ELO, level, K/D, headshot %, win rate, streaks, and recent form.

/ic stats /ic stats user:@Adam
/ic recent ✓ Available

Last 10 matches with map, score, W/L, kills, deaths, and K/D.

/ic recent /ic recent user:@Adam /ic recent count:20
/ic form ✓ Available

Aggregated performance summary over recent matches — W/L record, avg K/D, avg HS%, best and worst game, map breakdown.

/ic form /ic form user:@Adam /ic form matches:40

Group Statistics

/ic leaderboard ✓ Available

Insomniacs leaderboard. Use the dropdown to switch between ELO, K/D, win rate, headshots %, matches played, and best win streak.

/ic leaderboard
/ic compare ✓ Available

Side-by-side lifetime stats comparison between two linked players.

/ic compare player1:@Adam player2:@Dave

Maps

/ic maps ✓ Available

Per-map breakdown — games played, wins, losses, win rate, and K/D. Based on last 50 matches.

/ic maps /ic maps user:@Adam
/ic mapstats ✓ Available

Detailed statistics for a specific map — games, win rate, avg K/D, avg HS%, best game. Based on last 100 matches.

/ic mapstats map:Mirage /ic mapstats map:Ancient user:@Adam

Records & History

/ic records ✓ Available

Insomniacs all-time record book across all linked players — highest ELO, best K/D, best win rate, longest streak, most matches, best kill game.

/ic records
/ic session ✓ Available

Stats from the most recent play session — games, W/L, K/D, HS%, best game, maps played, and ELO change where available.

/ic session /ic session user:@Adam

Chemistry

/ic chemistry ✓ Available

Duo win rates with other linked players over the last 100 matches. Shows best duo, worst duo, most-played-with, and a full teammate table. Duos under 10 shared games are marked as small sample.

/ic chemistry /ic chemistry user:@Adam

Achievements

/ic achievements ✓ Available

Earned achievements calculated silently from match history — never announced. Includes Thirty Bomb, The Hard Carry, On Fire, Tilt Queue, Go To Bed, The Insomniac, Unstoppable, Ace, and more.

/ic achievements /ic achievements user:@Adam

General

/ic poll ✓ Available

Create a poll with up to 5 options. The bot adds emoji reactions for voting. Only the creator can end the poll early; clicking End Poll shows the final vote counts.

/ic poll question:Best map? option1:Mirage option2:Inferno /ic poll question:When? option1:Tonight option2:Tomorrow duration:48
/ic ping ✓ Available

Check bot status and latency. Shows round-trip and WebSocket ping.

/ic ping
Pull-based only

None of these commands post automatically. Every response requires a user to actively ask for it.
