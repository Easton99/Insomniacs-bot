Everything you need to settle the argument

From live ELO tracking to all-time records — the Insomniacs history book, automated.

FACEIT Account Linking

Link your Discord account to your FACEIT nickname once. The bot tracks matches automatically from that point on and backfills your history.

Rich Player Statistics

K/D, ADR, headshot %, win rate, ELO history, streaks, recent form — over any time period you choose.

Server Leaderboards

Who has the highest ELO? Best K/D? Most 30-bombs? The Insomniacs leaderboard covers the serious stats and the fun ones.

Match History

Browse recent games with map, score, kills, deaths, ADR, and ELO change for each match.

Insomniacs Record Book

All-time records for kills, ADR, streaks, biggest ELO sessions, and more — silently updated whenever the records change.

Teammate Chemistry

Which duo has the best win rate? Who is your best teammate? Find out who you actually perform with — with a minimum sample size to keep it honest.

Achievements

Thirty Bomb, The Hard Carry, Tilt Queue, Go To Bed — milestones are tracked silently and shown when you ask for them.

Server Quotes

Save the best (or worst) things people say with a right-click context menu command. Pull a random quote any time with /quote.

A few commands to get you started

All commands use Discord's slash command interface — just type / in any channel.

/stats Planned

View a full statistics embed for yourself or another player, with buttons to jump to Recent, Maps, and Records.

/stats /stats user:@Adam /stats user:@Adam period:month
/leaderboard Planned

The Insomniacs leaderboard — ranked by ELO, K/D, win rate, or any other supported category via a select menu.

/leaderboard
/ping ✓ Available

Check bot status and latency. Useful to confirm the bot is online and responding.

/ping

Map statistics that actually matter

See who dominates on Mirage, who drags the team down on Ancient, and which map the Insomniacs should never queue.

Per-player and group views

/maps shows the whole group's win rate per map. /mapstats map:Mirage user:@Adam drills into one player on one map — games played, K/D, ADR, best performance.
